# Getting started

Three short pages take you from nothing to your first picture:

1. **[Install and check your phone](install.md)** — getting the app, and finding out which
   models your chip can run.
2. **[Your first picture](first-picture.md)** — download one model, open a ready-made flow,
   press Run.
3. **[The canvas](canvas.md)** — what everything on the main screen does: nodes, wires,
   gestures, the run log.

If you have used ComfyUI before, the idea is the same: nodes joined by wires. The difference is
that each node here does a whole job (a "Text to image" node encodes the prompt, samples and
decodes by itself), so a complete flow is usually three or four nodes, not fifteen.
